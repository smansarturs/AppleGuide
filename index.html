⚠️ A Note on Refurbished Units

In 2026, we strongly advise against buying non-certified refurbished iPhones. Modern AI hardware requires precise calibration between the Neural Engine and the OS. Third-party refurbished units often use non-genuine parts that disable critical AI optimizations, leading to slower performance and reduced battery life in AI-heavy workloads.
