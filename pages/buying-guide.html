Buying Guide

Where better to buy the specific model?

Variations:

Base/Regular iPhones: Apple Store or Authorized Retail Stores

We all know that regular phones sells longer, than Pros, when new iPhone lineup has released. That's why better buy new iPhone from Authorized Retail Store or in Apple Store.

Pro iPhones: Used (But in Authorized Retail Stores)

Pro iPhones is cheaper, when you buy used, but only in ideal condition, and all functions are working, Face ID as example. You still get a Pro-functions, but with cheaper price.

DO NOT BUY REFURBISHED. Why?

Refurbished phones, especially non-certified by Apple, can contain non-original phone parts, which performance will be worse. Some functions may be missing, like True-Tone and Face ID, for example.
