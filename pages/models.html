Worth iPhone in 2026

Which one better to buy in 2026?

Top 10:

#10 - iPhone 13

It's most budget iPhone in 2026. Still capable in nowadays. Better buy iPhone 13 instead of next-year's model (iPhone 14)


#9 - iPhone 13 Pro/Pro Max

Most budget Pro iPhone. 120 Hz and ProMotion. A15 Bionic chip. That's also better than iPhone 14. Better camera.


#8 - iPhone 16e

Most affordable cheap iPhone in nowadays. A18 chip, old notch instead of Dynamic Island, non-MagSafe. iPhone 13/14 frame.


#7 - iPhone Air

Performance instead of autonomy. This iPhone have bad battery, and that's why iPhone Air is thin. A19 Pro chip, 12 GB RAM, single camera. Very powerful phone, despite the battery and single camera.


#6 - iPhone 17e

New model. This is same 16e, but with A19 Chip, with MagSafe. But still have notch and 60 Hz.


#5 - iPhone 15

Better than 13 and 14. First regular iPhone with Dynamic Island. First iPhone with USB-C. Cheap and good choice, if you want Dynamic Island instead of notch.


#4 - iPhone 14 Pro/Pro Max

First ever iPhone with Dynamic Island. Better than iPhone 15, because of triple camera and stainless steel frame, ProMotion and first iPhone with Always-On display


#3 - iPhone 15 Pro/Pro Max

One of the best iPhones. First iPhone, where can play AAA-games, because of A17 Pro chip. Titanium frame and ProMotion. Triple camera. Exclusive iPhone, which supports Apple Intelligence. First iPhone with Action Button instead of silent switch.


#2 - iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max

Newest iPhone now. A20 Pro chip and smaller Dynamic Island with 3 live activities. Better battery life, and Camera diaphragm.


#1 - iPhone 17

First regular iPhone with ProMotion and Always-On display. Supports Apple Intelligence, A19 Chip and double camera with 48 MP. Really worth to buy.
